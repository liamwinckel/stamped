# Stamped

A passport book for everywhere you've been. Every place becomes a one-of-a-kind ink stamp, with country colours, landmarks and skylines, a year in review, lifetime stats, photo memories and iCloud Drive backups.

Runs entirely on your phone: no accounts, no servers, works offline.

**Use it:** open https://liamwinckel.github.io/stamped in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
